# Relay marketing site

## System

- **Color:** warm paper `#F4F0E6`, secondary paper `#EAE4D6`, near-black ink `#12130F`, muted ink `#6B6A60`, hairline `#D3CCBC`, signal red `#A8431C`, and near-black product panels.
- **Type:** Inter for body and interface text, Instrument Serif for a small number of display emphasis moments, and JetBrains Mono for commands, statuses, and captions.
- **Grid:** centered 1280px container with 32px desktop gutters and 20px mobile gutters. Hero and dense content use an asymmetric 7/5 split; section headings use a 3/9 split.
- **Spacing:** 88–128px section rhythm, 1px rules between major areas, rectangular controls with 2px radius, and no card shadows.
- **Motion:** content is readable without JavaScript. Any future motion should be limited to short opacity/translate reveals and must collapse under reduced motion.

## Deliberate rejections

The redesign avoids stock photography, invented metrics, fake logos, gradient meshes, glass surfaces, decorative dashboard charts, autoplay motion, and cloud-software language. Product panels are built from the documented Relay workflow so the page demonstrates the product rather than implying it through abstract imagery.
